Subject Notes

Your complete guide to self-transformation.

🎯 High-Weightage Topics

  • Must Do Reading Comprehension — RC passages, inference questions
  • Must Do Grammar — Tenses, Voice, Narration, Articles
  • Must Do Error Detection & Sentence Improvement
  • Important Vocabulary — Synonyms, Antonyms, One-word Sub.
  • Important Idioms & Phrases, Cloze Test
  • Good to Know Para Jumbles & Para Completion

📚 Chapter-wise Notes

  • Simple Present: I write / She writes — habitual actions
  • Present Continuous: I am writing — action happening now
  • Present Perfect: I have written — recently completed action
  • Past Perfect: I had written — completed before another past action
  • Future Perfect: I will have written — completed before a future time
  • Tip: Learn signal words: already, yet, since, for, just, ever, never
  • Active: Subject performs the action — She reads the book
  • Passive: Subject receives the action — The book is read by her
  • Formula: Object + helping verb (be) + V3 + by + Subject
  • Modals in Passive: can/could/may/must + be + V3
  • Tip: Identify V3 form of the verb and place it after "been/be/being"
  • Reporting Verb: Said, told, asked, ordered, exclaimed
  • Tense Change: Present → Past; Present Perfect → Past Perfect
  • Pronoun Change: I → he/she; We → they; You → he/she/they
  • Time Change: Now → then; Today → that day; Tomorrow → next day
  • Questions: Use "if/whether" for yes-no questions in indirect speech
  • Subject-Verb Agreement: Singular subject → singular verb (Everyone is…)
  • Pronoun Errors: Each/Everyone + singular pronoun (his/her, not their)
  • Misplaced Modifiers: Modifier must be next to what it modifies
  • Preposition Errors: Interested in, tired of, afraid of, differ from
  • Redundancy: Avoid "revert back", "future plans", "past history"
  • Abate: Synonym: decrease, Antonym: increase, intensify
  • Benevolent: Synonym: kind/generous, Antonym: malevolent/cruel
  • Candid: Synonym: frank/honest, Antonym: dishonest/evasive
  • Diligent: Synonym: hardworking, Antonym: lazy/idle
  • Tip: Learn root words: bene (good), mal (bad), ante (before), post (after)
  • Idiom: "Bite the bullet" = endure pain; "Break the ice" = start conversation

✏️ My Personal Notes — Hindi

🎯 High-Weightage Topics

  • Must Do Series — Number, Letter, Mixed Series
  • Must Do Coding-Decoding & Analogy
  • Must Do Blood Relations & Direction-Distance
  • Important Seating Arrangement & Puzzles
  • Important Syllogism & Statement-Conclusion
  • Good to Know Non-Verbal — Mirror Image, Paper Folding

📚 Chapter-wise Notes

  • Arithmetic Series: Common difference — 2, 5, 8, 11, 14 (diff = 3)
  • Geometric Series: Common ratio — 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 (ratio = 2)
  • Square Series: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36... (n²)
  • Cube Series: 1, 8, 27, 64, 125... (n³)
  • Mixed Series: Combine two patterns; find both and apply
  • Letter Series: A=1, B=2... Z=26. AZBYCX — find the pattern!
  • Letter Coding: Each letter shifted by fixed value — A→D, B→E (+3)
  • Reverse Coding: A=Z, B=Y, C=X... opposite end
  • Number Coding: A=1, B=2 or A=2, B=3 (position-based)
  • Word Coding: Rearranged or substituted words — find the rule
  • Tip: Always find the pattern with given examples before solving
  • Key Terms: Son/Daughter, Father/Mother, Brother/Sister, Nephew/Niece
  • Paternal: Father's side — Grandfather, Uncle (Father's brother = Paternal uncle)
  • Maternal: Mother's side — Maternal uncle, Maternal aunt
  • In-laws: Father-in-law, Mother-in-law, Son-in-law, Daughter-in-law
  • Tip: Draw a family tree. Use M/F symbols. Arrows show relationships.
  • Main Directions: N, S, E, W — NE, NW, SE, SW as diagonals
  • Left Turn: Rotate 90° anti-clockwise; Right Turn: 90° clockwise
  • Shadow Rule: Morning sun = East (shadow falls West); Evening = West (shadow East)
  • Final Distance: Use Pythagoras theorem if perpendicular movements
  • Tip: Always draw a diagram and mark each move carefully
  • All A are B: A is fully inside B (Venn diagram)
  • No A is B: A and B have no intersection
  • Some A are B: Partial overlap between A and B
  • Some A are not B: At least part of A is outside B
  • Tip: Check all possibilities with Venn diagrams; don't assume
  • Either-Or Cases: When neither conclusion follows alone but both together do

✏️ My Personal Notes — Reasoning

🎯 High-Weightage Topics

  • Must Do Percentage, Profit & Loss — most common in exams
  • Must Do Simple & Compound Interest — formula-based
  • Must Do Time, Speed & Distance & Time & Work
  • Important Ratio, Proportion & Averages
  • Important Data Interpretation — Tables, Bar, Pie charts
  • Good to Know Geometry, Mensuration & Trigonometry

📚 Chapter-wise Notes

  • % to Fraction: 25% = 1/4; 33.33% = 1/3; 12.5% = 1/8
  • % Change: (New - Old) / Old × 100
  • Ratio a:b = c:d: Cross multiplication — ad = bc
  • Compound Ratio: a:b and c:d → ac:bd
  • Tip: Memorize fraction-% equivalents up to 1/12 for speed!
  • Profit %: (SP - CP) / CP × 100
  • Loss %: (CP - SP) / CP × 100
  • SP = CP × (100 + P%) / 100
  • Discount: On Marked Price; SP = MP × (100 - D%) / 100
  • Successive Discount: x% and y% = x + y - xy/100
  • Tip: When two items sold at same SP with equal % profit & loss → always a loss!
  • SI = PRT/100 (P = Principal, R = Rate, T = Time in years)
  • CI = P(1 + R/100)^T - P
  • CI - SI for 2 years = P(R/100)²
  • Effective Rate for 2 years: R + R + R²/100
  • Tip: For CI, multiply P by (1 + R/100) for each year
  • Formula: Speed = Distance / Time; D = S × T; T = D / S
  • Unit Conversion: km/h to m/s: multiply by 5/18; m/s to km/h: multiply by 18/5
  • Relative Speed: Same direction = S1 - S2; Opposite = S1 + S2
  • Train Problems: Time = (Length of train + Length of platform) / Speed
  • Average Speed: 2ab/(a+b) when equal distances at speeds a and b
  • If A does work in n days: A's 1-day work = 1/n
  • A + B together: 1/A + 1/B = 1/Total Days
  • Pipes & Cisterns: Inlet fills (+), Outlet empties (-)
  • Work & Wages: Wages proportional to work done (not days if efficiency differs)
  • Tip: Use LCM method — assume total work = LCM of individual days

✏️ My Personal Notes — Quantitative Aptitude

🎯 High-Weightage Topics

  • Must Do X-ray Physics — Production, Properties, Interactions
  • Must Do Radiographic Positioning — All body parts
  • Must Do Radiation Safety & Protection — ALARA principle
  • Important Anatomy for Radiographers — Skeletal & Organ systems
  • Important Darkroom Technology & Film Processing
  • Good to Know CT, MRI, Ultrasound — Basic Principles

📚 Chapter-wise Notes

  • X-ray Tube: Cathode (filament) emits electrons; Anode (tungsten target) produces X-rays
  • Bremsstrahlung: Braking radiation — electron decelerates near nucleus
  • Characteristic Radiation: Electron displaces inner-shell electron of target atom
  • kVp: Controls X-ray quality (penetration); mAs controls quantity (density)
  • Properties of X-rays: Travel at speed of light, not deflected by magnetic fields, cause ionization
  • Tip: kVp ↑ = more penetration; mAs ↑ = more density on film
  • PA Chest: Posteroanterior; patient faces the film; shows heart size accurately
  • AP View: Anteroposterior; beam enters from front; used when PA not possible
  • Lateral: Patient sideways; left or right lateral specified
  • Oblique: Patient rotated at angle (e.g., RAO, LAO, RPO, LPO)
  • Skull Views: PA, Lateral, Towne's (occipital bone), Waters (PNS)
  • Tip: Learn standard views, SID (Source-to-Image Distance), and OFD for each region
  • ALARA: As Low As Reasonably Achievable — guiding principle
  • Annual Dose Limit: Occupational = 20 mSv/year; Public = 1 mSv/year
  • Protective Devices: Lead apron (0.25-0.5 mm Pb), thyroid collar, gonad shields
  • Inverse Square Law: Intensity ∝ 1/distance² — double distance = quarter intensity
  • Monitoring: TLD (Thermoluminescent Dosimeter), Film badge, Pocket dosimeter
  • Safelight: Red/amber light used in darkroom — does not expose film
  • Processing Steps: Developer → Stop Bath → Fixer → Wash → Dry
  • Developer: Reduces exposed silver halide crystals to metallic silver (creates the image)
  • Fixer: Removes unexposed silver halide; makes image permanent
  • Automatic Processor: Developer temp ~35°C; Total processing time ~90 seconds
  • Fluoroscopy: Real-time X-ray imaging; used for GI studies (Barium swallow, enema)
  • CT Scan: Multiple X-ray slices; cross-sectional images; HU (Hounsfield Units) scale
  • MRI: Uses magnetic field + radio waves; no radiation; best for soft tissue
  • Ultrasound: High-frequency sound waves; real-time imaging; safe in pregnancy
  • Contrast Media: Barium sulfate (GI), Iodine-based (IV & vascular); positive/negative contrast
